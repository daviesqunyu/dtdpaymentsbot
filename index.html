Help

FAQ

Payments, delivery, Telegram bot, and support — short answers only.

USDT TRC20 Bitcoin Paystack @Dtdpaymentbot
How do I pay?

Preferred: USDT on Tron (TRC20) — scan the QR or copy the address, send the exact USD amount, then paste the tx hash. Also: Bitcoin, Telegram Stars ⭐ (in Mini App / bot), or Paystack (card, bank, or M-Pesa in KES). Keep your Order ID.

How do you earn with the bot (Stars / ads)?

Customers can pay with Telegram Stars. Stars land on your bot balance — open the bot’s Balance / Monetization in Telegram to Buy Ads or withdraw via Fragment (TON). Share your referral link from /refer in @Dtdpaymentbot. Full playbook: /earn.

How does crypto checkout work with the bot?

In @Dtdpaymentbot: tap a product → choose USDT (TRC20) or Bitcoin → scan the QR / copy the address → send payment → paste your transaction hash → send your delivery email. Same flow on the website checkout.

How fast is delivery?

Digital delivery starts after payment confirmation. Updates go to Telegram and email.

Can I order from Telegram?

Yes — open @Dtdpaymentbot and use /buy. Track with /order.

Who supports DTD Store?

Supported internationally by DV Technologies.

What is the SMTP product?

SMTP unlocks the store console at #smtp for sending email from contact@dvtechnologies.xyz (VPS Postfix) and SMS to phone numbers (VPS worker; Telegram notifies admin). Unlock with your paid Order ID + checkout email. Admin accounts always have access.

Who do I contact for support?

Email contact@dvtechnologies.xyz for tracking, payments, and channel questions — or message @Davetheedev on Telegram.
