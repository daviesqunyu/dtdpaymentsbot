FAQ

Payments, delivery, Telegram bot, and support — short answers only.

USDT TRC20 Bitcoin Paystack @DTDSTOREBOT
How do I pay?

Recommended: global Card / Bank through Paystack. Card details stay on Paystack’s secure hosted checkout. Also: Bitcoin, Telegram Stars ⭐, or global Card / Bank checkout through Paystack. Prices are in USD.

How do you earn with the bot (Stars / ads)?

Customers can pay with Telegram Stars. Stars land on your bot balance — open the bot’s Balance / Monetization in Telegram to Buy Ads or withdraw via Fragment (TON). Share your referral link from /refer in @DTDSTOREBOT. Full playbook: /earn.

How does crypto checkout work with the bot?

In @DTDSTOREBOT: tap a product → open the official DTD Payment page → confirm your delivery email → choose Card / Bank (recommended) or optional crypto. The same Order ID tracks either flow.

How fast is delivery?

Digital delivery starts after payment confirmation. Updates go to Telegram and email.

Can I order from Telegram?

Yes — open @DTDSTOREBOT and use /buy. Track with /order.

Who supports DTD Store?

Supported internationally by DV Technologies.

What is the SMTP product?

SMTP unlocks the store console at #smtp for sending email from contact@dvtechnologies.xyz (VPS Postfix) and SMS to phone numbers (VPS worker; Telegram notifies admin). Unlock with your paid Order ID + checkout email. Admin accounts always have access.

Who do I contact for support?

Email contact@dvtechnologies.xyz for tracking, payments, and channel questions — or message vendor @Glock7money · owner @Glock7money on Telegram.
